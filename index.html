- Overview -

Calvin Coolidge was the president of the US from 1923 to 1929. He first served as vice president to President Warren G. Harding, from 1920 to 1923. He took over after Harding’s death in 1923. During his presidency he was known for being a man of action and stability. After he left office, he became known as the “Do Nothing” president. He was quite popular, and was known for being laconic, nicknamed “Silent Cal”.
